# Notice

The teaching method in `skills/` draws on these projects. The text was rewritten for this plugin.

- **janestreet/agent_skills, `socratic-tutor`** (MIT). The tutor mode adapts its structure: diagnostic placement, a 0 to 5 skill map, one question per message, strict level rules, spaced review and a final assessment. Its licence follows.
- **DrCatHicks/learning-opportunities** (CC BY 4.0, Dr. Cat Hicks). The exercise types (predict, generate, trace, teach back) and the rule of stopping at the question.
- **cskwork/supertutor-skill** (MIT). The idea that mastery needs unseen transfer problems.
- **meta-agentic/meta-discipline-math** (MIT). The shape of the subject cards: method, checks and typical traps.
- **G. Polya, How to Solve It**. The problem-solving card.
